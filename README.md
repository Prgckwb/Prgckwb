## About Me

Machine Learning Engineer at **CyberAgent** (Shibuya, Tokyo), specializing in **Computer Vision** and **image generation**.
I studied at the [**Yanai Lab**](https://mm.cs.uec.ac.jp/), The University of Electro-Communications (UEC), where I researched **Diffusion Models** and **Zero-shot Semantic Segmentation**.

- 💼 ML Engineer @ CyberAgent — computer vision for advertising
- 🎓 Alumnus of Yanai Lab, UEC
- 🏅 **Kaggle Competitions Master** — 🥇 ×1 / 🥈 ×4 / 🥉 ×5
